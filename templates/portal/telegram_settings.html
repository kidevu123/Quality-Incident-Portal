Staff "I'm on this claim"

Quick acknowledgment from Telegram or the workspace.

In the ticket workspace, drop 👁 or 👀 in a reply or internal note to ping the team on Telegram, add the acceptance note, move New → Open when needed, and assign yourself if the ticket has no assignee.

In Telegram, react with 👀 / 👁 on a claim alert, or reply with those emojis (or include the ticket id). The bot webhook must include message_reaction in allowed_updates — run python manage.py telegram_set_webhook after deploy.

Telegram pings and the auto internal note are rate-limited to about once per 10 minutes per person per ticket; status and assignee still update every time.
